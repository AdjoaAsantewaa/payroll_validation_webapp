# Payroll FAQ

## Do I need to fix every issue before I can submit?

No. You can submit with issues still open -- the submission goes to the
Payroll Specialist for review either way. It is better to fix what you can
first, since that reduces back-and-forth, but it is not required.

## What happens when I upload a corrected file?

The new file becomes the current version of your submission for this
cycle. It is checked from scratch, and the previous version is kept as
history but no longer counts toward your open issues.

## An issue says my overtime looks unusual -- is my file wrong?

Not necessarily. Unusual overtime is flagged because it is far above an
employee's normal average, not because it is outside the allowed range. If
the figure is correct, explain it rather than changing it. If it was a
mistake, correct it and resubmit.

## Why was my employee flagged as unknown?

Their Staff ID does not match anyone in the employee master data for your
department. Double-check the ID for typos. If they are a genuinely new
employee, the employee master data needs to be updated first -- this is
not something you can fix from the spreadsheet alone.

## Can I see another department's payroll data?

No. Each Department Submitter only ever sees their own department's
submissions, employees, and issues.

## What does the Payroll Specialist do with my submission?

They review each flagged issue, decide whether to accept it, reject it, or
ask you to confirm or correct it, and approve the submission once
everything is resolved. Approved submissions are included in the next
export to payroll.

## I answered a query -- what happens next?

Your answer is recorded against that item. If you said the value is
correct, the Payroll Specialist reviews and decides whether to accept it.
If you said you would resubmit, upload the corrected file as normal.

## Who can approve a submission?

Only the Payroll Specialist. A submitter cannot approve their own
submission, and the assistant cannot approve it either -- approval is
always a human decision made in the Exceptions screen.
